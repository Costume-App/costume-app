# House Inventory, VO script

Voice: Ava (en-US-AvaMultilingualNeural). Recorded against the Demo Theatre Co. org.

## House Inventory (`intro`)

In the last video, we logged a finished doublet into House Inventory. House Inventory is your costume library: everything your company already owns, photographed and easy to find, so you can reuse it instead of making it again. In this video, we will look at how it is organized, add a brand new item from scratch, and then pull an existing item straight into a different show, exactly the kind of thing House Inventory is built for.

## Finding it (`find-inventory`)

Open it from "Inventory" at the top of any page.

> point at the nav link "Inventory"

It also has its own card on the Productions page, which shows how many items you have on hand. That count grows every time you log a finished piece, so it is always an honest picture of what is on the shelf.

> on /productions: zoom the "House Inventory →" card with its count; then click through to /inventory

## The photo grid (`the-grid`)

Every item shows as a photo tile, with its size and how many you have. Items are grouped by category, and you can fold a category away when you do not need it.

> hold on the grid; zoom one tile's "size · ×qty" line; click the "Hats" header to collapse, then again to expand

To find something fast, type in the search box, and only the matching items and their categories stay on screen, so a big library never feels overwhelming.

> type "hat" in "Search inventory…"; hold on the match count; clear it

## Add an item (`add-item`)

To add something new, choose "Add item" and give it a name.

> click "+ Add item"; type "Velvet cloak"; click "Add item"

Then fill in the details: a category, the size, how many you have, and where it is stored. Each field saves the moment you click away, with nothing extra to submit, and there is a notes box too, for anything else worth remembering.

> "Category" type "Coats & capes"; "Size" "M"; "Quantity" 2; "Location" "Rack A"; hold on "Saved ✓"

Add up to six photos, so anyone on your team can recognize it at a glance.

> photo button; file chooser gets velvet-cloak.jpg; hold on the thumbnail; click "Done"

## An item's details (`item-details`)

Tap any tile to open it. Here is the doublet from the last video. The app remembers which production and role it was made for, so you never lose track of where a piece first came from.

> click the "Doublet" tile (Uncategorized group); zoom "Made for: Twelfth Night → Viola"

Everything stays editable, so you can file it under a category now, the same way you filled one in a moment ago. You can also update its size, quantity, or storage location any time something changes, and the same "Saved" flash confirms every edit.

> "Category": type "Doublets"; Tab; hold on "Saved ✓"

## Reuse it in a show (`reuse`)

This is the payoff for keeping that library up to date. Open a role's "Costume" tab, and choose "add from inventory" instead of building the piece from scratch.

> Twelfth Night, Sebastian row, "Costume"; click "+ add from inventory"

Search, then pick the item; each result shows its category right alongside its name. It is added as a costume piece, with its photos, and it is marked "On hand" for the performer, with its storage location right beside it, so nobody has to ask where it lives.

> type "coat" in the picker; click "Pirate coat"; click the new "Pirate coat" row to expand it; zoom its photo; zoom Jordan Lee's row: source "On hand", "Rack A"

## Check it without leaving (`peek-and-used-in`)

Choose "From inventory" to look at the item without leaving the page. It shows the same photo and details as the full item page, without pulling you away from the role you are working on.

> click "From inventory ↗"; hold on the peek dialog (photo, "Coats & capes · L · ×3", "Location: Rack A")

And "Open in inventory" takes you to the item itself, which now shows where it is being used, the same way it showed what it was made for a moment ago. It is the same item page from earlier in this video, just with one more relationship added to it.

> click "Open in inventory ↗"; the item opens expanded; zoom "Used in: Twelfth Night → Sebastian"

## Wrap up (`wrap-up`)

That is House Inventory: photograph what you own, find it in seconds, and pull it straight into your next show instead of building it all over again. Whatever you catalog today saves time on every production that comes after this one, and it only takes a minute to add.
